# S2 specification and prospective methodology

Written before S2 development outcomes. S1 source and evidence remain archived.

## Question, scope and contribution

Does a regression predictor constrained to be linear in forcing reduce correction iterations beyond strong classical starting points, at equal true-residual tolerance? We study synthetic scalar diffusion on [0,1], homogeneous Dirichlet boundaries, arithmetic face coefficients, and grids of 65 and 129 total points. This is a controlled empirical audit of an established hybrid-solver idea, not a new neural architecture or state-of-the-art claim. Predictor inputs are four coefficient coordinates c and four low-frequency source coordinates f. OOD forcing can contain four extra frequencies, which all solvers receive in the full RHS but the fixed predictor cannot represent.

Existing quadratic warm starts struggle with strict residual acceptance under diagonal preconditioning. We propose a source-linear polynomial predictor because linearity of the elliptic solution map in forcing predicts exact superposition and removal of spurious forcing-quadratic terms. This principle guarantees a structural property, not an iteration or runtime improvement. Positive evidence must survive classical controls.

## Mathematics and chronological computation

1. Sample c_j uniformly on [-s,s], s=0.45 IID and s=0.9 coefficient OOD; sample f_j uniformly on [-1,1]. Set a(x)=exp(sum(c_j sin(j pi x))) and b(x)=sum(f_j sin(j pi x)). OOD forcing adds independently seeded modes 5--8. Sample at x_i=i/(N-1). The data are dimensionless, synthetic, generated locally/hosted; no external dataset or real-world measurement is claimed.
2. Face coefficients are a_(i+1/2)=(a_i+a_(i+1))/2. For n=N-2 interior coordinates, A has diagonal h^-2(a_(i-1/2)+a_(i+1/2)) and neighboring entries -h^-2 a_(i+1/2). A is SPD because v^T A v=h^-2 sum a_face (v_(i+1)-v_i)^2>0 for v nonzero with zero boundaries.
3. Training targets are exact-to-floating-point discrete solutions from Thomas elimination. Each training replicate receives its own 192 independently seeded cases. There is no iterative neural training and hence no epoch curve. Fit the linear coefficients of a fixed nonlinear feature map.
4. Quadratic control: x=[c,f] in R^8, phi_q=[1,x,{x_i x_j:i<=j}] in R^45. Source-linear ablation: phi_1=[1,c] tensor f in R^20. Proposed predictor: phi_2=[1,c,{c_i c_j:i<=j}] tensor f in R^60. All maps output interior vectors uhat=phi W, W in R^(p x n); exterior values are exactly zero. A shuffled-label control has identical p=60, data, fit budget and implementation with training targets permuted.
5. Fit W=argmin ||XW-Y||_F^2 + tr(W^T Lambda W), ridge 1e-4. Lambda is ridge I except the quadratic intercept uses ridge*1e-6 to match S1. Compute one multi-RHS augmented least-squares factorization [X;sqrt(Lambda)]W=[Y;0] via numpy.linalg.lstsq. No target-derived scaling, test fitting or online updates. Save all W and training-case identities. Complexity O(mp^2+p^3+mpn+p^2n), inference O(pn), storage O(pn).
6. Source linearity proof: for fixed c, phi(c,alpha f+beta g)=alpha phi(c,f)+beta phi(c,g), hence the prediction obeys exact superposition in exact arithmetic and predicts zero at zero source. This does not bound its coefficient extrapolation error or guarantee fewer Krylov steps.
7. Correct with PCG. Compare diagonal M=diag(A) with constant-Laplacian M=T, T tridiagonal (-1,2,-1). Each solve constructs its own preconditioner; factor T once per solve and use forward/back substitution at each step. The constant h^-2 scaling cancels in PCG. A has Rayleigh quotient relative to h^-2 T in [min(a_face),max(a_face)], so the preconditioned condition-number bound is at most max(a_face)/min(a_face), independent of grid size at fixed coefficient bounds. This is a classical fact, not a learning contribution.
8. PCG recurrence uses r=b-Ax, z=M^-1r, p=z, alpha=(r^Tz)/(p^TAp), x+=alpha p, r-=alpha Ap, beta=(r_new^Tz_new)/(r_old^Tz_old). Reject nonpositive/nonfinite curvature. At recursive convergence recompute b-Ax; if it fails, restart from the actual residual. Stop at max(1e-12,rtol*||b||_2) or 4n iterations. Every returned solution gets an additional independent true-residual acceptance check.
9. Physics-only initial guess solves the constant-coefficient PDE using mean face diffusivity: x0=(h^-2 mean(a_face) T)^-1 b. Direct Thomas solution is the mandatory task-appropriate baseline. There is no defensible reason to claim general superiority over direct O(n) solving in one dimension without measured evidence.

## Matrix and controls

Nine methods: direct; zero/Jacobi; quadratic/Jacobi; zero/Laplacian; physics/Laplacian; quadratic/Laplacian; linear/Laplacian; structured/Laplacian; shuffled/Laplacian. Mechanism-off controls separate preconditioning, source-structure and learned pairing. The shuffled control is parameter and training-compute matched. Quadratic and structured feature counts differ; their contrast is not an isolated capacity-controlled causal effect. No FNO/HINTS/SOTA reproduction is claimed.

Two grids, two tolerances (1e-6 and 1e-10), three regimes (IID, doubled coefficient range, unseen forcing modes). Full cross-product; no excluded failures. Development: three training replicates, 12 evaluation cases per cell, two timing passes. Confirmation: five training replicates, 32 cases per cell, three timing passes. Resolution comparisons use separately fitted models; they are not zero-shot grid transfer. Forcing OOD is a specified perturbation test, not arbitrary corruption robustness. Candidate 1 is degree one (ablation); candidate 2 is degree two (proposed). No additional model search is authorized by this protocol.

## Seed, timing, uncertainty and decisions

Development seed base 1,000,000; confirmation 3,000,000; engineering smoke 9,000,000. Replicate r adds 100,000r and grid-index g adds 10,000g. Train uses offset 0; regime j uses offset 1000(j+1). Counts below 1000 are enforced. Shuffling uses train base+999. Forcing shifts use seed+7,000,000. Independent experimental units for model-level uncertainty are training/evaluation blocks; cases within a trained model are paired observations, not independent model fits.

Primary cell: IID, grid 129, relative residual tolerance 1e-6. Primary claim requires proposed mean paired relative iteration reduction >=15% and bootstrap 95% lower endpoint >=5% against BOTH zero/Laplacian and physics/Laplacian, with all compared solves converged. The conjunction prevents selecting whichever baseline is weaker; no multiple exploratory cell is promoted to confirmation. Bootstrap resamples five block means (10,000 repeats). Five blocks give weak tail resolution; report each block, call intervals descriptive, and make no asymptotic p-value claim. Strict-tolerance, shifts, scaling and all other comparisons are descriptive. Failure of this predeclared rule yields NEGATIVE for this claim, regardless of favorable secondary results.

Per-case online timing uses median of all randomized-order passes, includes assembly, prediction, preconditioner setup, correction and independent acceptance, and excludes data generation/model loading/training. Offline data-generation and fit time, parameter count, CPU time and peak RSS are reported separately. Training amortization is not assumed. Threads are limited to one. Direct solve has zero Krylov iterations; its iteration reduction is undefined and only time/accuracy comparisons are meaningful. Timing is platform-specific, not a universal speed claim. Methods share implementation choices; independent dense-reference checks address numerical correctness but do not establish implementation-optimal performance.

All raw inputs, solutions, histories, timing passes, fits and environments are retained. Each run snapshots source digests and refuses an existing output directory. Confirmatory execution requires matching frozen source and config digests; a changed scientific specification needs a successor. A verifier checks hashes, complete case-condition coverage, regenerated inputs, actual residuals/errors, timing medians and regenerated statistics. No automatic re-run for a positive outcome.

## Budgets and readiness

One development matrix and one confirmation matrix; maximum two explicitly specified candidate feature families. Hosted development budget <=1200 CPU seconds, confirmation <=1800, combined submission cap <=3600 CPU seconds plus bounded verification. No GPU/paid resources. Source and all required tests must pass in a bounded dry run before full execution. Runtime-budget failures are INCONCLUSIVE_RESOURCE, not negative efficacy. Preserve failures and output identity. No local full run while below the 512 MiB/stable-pageout guard.

## Limitations and terminal conditions

This study cannot establish novelty, real-data benefit, multidimensional scaling, calibrated uncertainty, neural-operator superiority or independent reproduction by another investigator. S1 pooled-point calibration is not used for S2, and its distribution-free claim is withdrawn. Finish when a valid bounded question has a defensible disposition and traceable artifacts; external publication/authorship approval and independent human reproduction are separate statuses.
