# Statistical validation

## Estimand and units

The primary endpoint is the equal-series mean of timestamp-paired scaled
absolute-error differences, MASE(DDFS) minus MASE(fixed share). Each series uses a
seasonal-naive scale computed only from its 2015 warm-up segment. Series, not
timestamps, receive equal weight.

## Dependence handling

The ten regional load series are date-aligned and correlated. The registered
bootstrap therefore uses common seven-day moving-block starts across every series,
preserving shared temporal shocks before equal-series aggregation. It uses 5,000
draws and seed 20261021. An IID interval and independently resampled series would not
represent the registered dependence structure.

## Independent recomputation

`scripts/verify_development_v2.py` uses only the Python standard library. From the
3,660 raw prediction rows it independently checks:

- archive and inner evidence hashes;
- exact series, method, timestamp, and row denominators;
- MAE, MASE, RMSE, bias, interval coverage, and interval width;
- all eight equal-series mean MASE values;
- all six registered per-series and aggregate point contrasts;
- series-win counts, relative improvement, and both decision gates.

The receipt reports `valid: true` with an empty issue list. The bootstrap confidence
intervals were not regenerated by this independent verifier; they remain outputs of
the hosted registered implementation, whose statistical fixtures passed the hosted
test suite.

## Decision

The primary estimate is +0.0031951934 MASE, its retained interval lies entirely above
zero, and DDFS wins on zero series. The practical threshold required an estimate no
greater than -0.01 plus at least 1% relative improvement. The mechanism gate also
required a favorable DDFS-minus-adaptive-share effect and majority consistency. Both
gates fail without borderline interpretation.

These are development-stage selection decisions, not confirmatory p-values.
