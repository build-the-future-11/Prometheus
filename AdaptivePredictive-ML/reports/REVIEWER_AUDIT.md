# Skeptical reviewer audit

## Verdict

The repository supports a careful development-stage negative result. It does not yet
support a competitive conference submission centered on a positive method.

## Strengths

- Exact causal predict-then-update ordering and identical expert-bank controls.
- A $2\times2$ factorial ablation that separates adaptive rate from directed target.
- A tuning-free AdaHedge comparator on the same expert bank.
- Ten complete chronological series with no hidden denominator.
- Synchronized block inference that preserves temporal and cross-series dependence.
- Exact source, raw-result, and manuscript asset identities.
- Independent standard-library recomputation of every reported point result.
- Uniformly unfavorable outcomes preserved without threshold changes or protected
  test inspection.

## Critical weaknesses

1. The contribution is incremental relative to fixed share and Markov expert priors.
2. All ten series belong to one correlated electricity domain.
3. BOA-class and modern deep online systems were not reproduced under a matched
   protocol.
4. No matched latency, memory, or operation measurement supports an efficiency claim.
5. No external researcher independently replayed the package.
6. The evidence is development-stage; protected confirmation was correctly stopped
   after the gate failed.

## Claim decisions

- Implementation and executed-source identity: supported.
- DDFS predictive benefit: contradicted in development.
- Directed-target mechanism benefit: contradicted in development.
- Generalization, efficiency, SOTA, and independent reproducibility: unsupported.
- Submission readiness: no.

## Required path for future work

Any continuation should be a separately registered successor with a scientific reason
that does not arise from opportunistic protected-test search. A publishable positive
method would need cross-domain replication, stronger matched baselines, resource
measurements, frozen confirmation, and independent reproduction. Revision 2 itself is
closed.
